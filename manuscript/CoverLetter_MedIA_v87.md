# Cover letter - Medical Image Analysis

Dear Editors,

We submit "Structural priors versus learned models in longitudinal post-treatment brain-tumour MRI: a multi-cohort empirical benchmark with seed and architecture robustness" for consideration as an original research article in *Medical Image Analysis*.

This manuscript addresses a practical failure mode in medical-AI benchmarking: model rankings can change when endpoint composition, cohort provenance and transfer direction change. Across four independent longitudinal post-treatment brain-tumour MRI cohorts, we compare a closed-form heat-kernel structural prior against lightweight 3D U-Net, residual U-Net, UNETR and SwinUNETR baselines. The central contribution is not a universal winner claim. Instead, the paper provides a reproducible regime-conditioned benchmark showing when ranking importation is unsafe, with UCSD-PTGBM retained as a counterexample to a pi-only rule.

The v87 submission package includes a claim-audited transformer comparability analysis correcting the padded-grid denominator issue, 56 references, five main figures, 16 Extended Data figures, and all source-data links required to reproduce the headline claims. The manuscript explicitly states that full-resolution nnU-Net and prospective clinical-utility validation remain future external-validation requirements.

The work has not been published or submitted elsewhere. All data used are public or derived from the project datasets described in the manuscript. Author metadata, ORCID, acknowledgements and institutional statements should be finalised before portal submission.

Sincerely,

Sheikh Kamrul Islam
